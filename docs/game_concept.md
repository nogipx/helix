## Terminal Hack Mini-Game Concept

### Lore Context

In the world of *Quantum Realms*, players find themselves in a futuristic society where technology is both a blessing and a curse. The players are cybernetically enhanced agents who specialize in infiltrating high-security systems to extract crucial information. Each player has unique skills and a backstory, but their shared goal is to outsmart the AI guardians and maintain the balance between technology and humanity.

### Game Mechanics

The *Terminal Hack Mini-Game* is a key component of the players' missions. It takes place on a simulated terminal screen, where players must input commands to bypass security protocols, access restricted data, and avoid detection.

#### Objectives
- **Objective 1:** Crack the terminal's login screen to gain access.
- **Objective 2:** Navigate through the system, avoiding security measures like firewalls and AI patrols.
- **Objective 3:** Collect valuable data without triggering an alarm.
- **Objective 4:** Maintain stealth to avoid being detected by the AI.

#### Mechanics
1. **Terminal Interface:** The game starts with a login screen. Players must correctly enter a series of commands to proceed. Each command is represented by a series of letters and numbers, with a limited number of attempts to get it right.
2. **Command Input:** Players input commands based on their agent's unique skills. For example, a hacker with a 'Phantom' skill might be able to bypass security more effectively. Incorrect commands result in a countdown, and after a certain number of incorrect attempts, the terminal locks down, requiring a restart.
3. **System Navigation:** Once logged in, players must navigate the system's hierarchy. Each level has security measures that must be bypassed. For instance, a firewall might require a specific sequence of commands, while an AI patrol might require stealth tactics to avoid detection.
4. **Data Collection:** Throughout the game, players can collect data by running specific commands. These data points are crucial for completing the mission. However, collecting too much data too quickly can trigger an alarm, leading to a failed mission.
5. **Stealth Mechanic:** Players must manage their stealth levels. High stealth allows them to bypass more security measures, but high risk means they are more likely to be detected. The goal is to balance between security and risk to complete the mission successfully.

#### Rewards and Penalties
- **Success:** Completing the mini-game successfully rewards the player with additional data, mission points, and a reputation boost within the community.
- **Failure:** Failing the mini-game penalizes the player with a loss of data, mission points, and a temporary decrease in their reputation.

### Conclusion

The *Terminal Hack Mini-Game* adds depth and variety to the gameplay, providing players with a unique challenge that tests their skills and strategies. It integrates seamlessly into the game's narrative and enhances the immersion by giving players a taste of the high-stakes technology they operate within *Quantum Realms*.
